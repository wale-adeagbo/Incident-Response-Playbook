# Communications templates

**Do not post externally** until Comms + (if applicable) Legal approve. Replace placeholders in brackets.

## Internal — incident declared

> **Incident:** [short name]  
> **Severity:** [SEV-x]  
> **IC:** [name]  
> **Customer impact:** [none / partial / major — be factual]  
> **Status:** [investigating / mitigating / monitoring]  
> **Next update:** [time TZ]

## Internal — all-clear (initial)

> **Incident:** [name]  
> **Status:** Mitigated / service restored for [scope].  
> **IC:** [name]  
> **Follow-up:** Post-incident review [date or “to be scheduled”].

## Customer / status page (short)

> We are investigating [issue affecting X]. We will update this page by [time].  
> [Link to status page]

## Customer / status page (resolved)

> The issue affecting [X] has been resolved. We apologize for the disruption.  
> A summary will be posted within [YOUR_POLICY_WINDOW].

## Security note (internal only, pre-approval)

> We are investigating **potential unauthorized access**. Do not speculate publicly.  
> Route all external inquiries to [comms/legal]. Preserve logs per [runbook link].
